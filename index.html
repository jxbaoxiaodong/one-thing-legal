Privacy Policy · 隐私政策 · Last updated 2026-11-24

What we collect

  • A pseudonym you choose.
  • A random device-generated identifier used to keep you signed in.
  • The content you post: type, pseudonym of the person involved (if any), the story, and your one-sentence reflection.
  • Reports you submit and authors you block.
  • The time each post or report was created.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Why we collect it

  • To show posts to other users.
  • To keep you signed in on this device.
  • To review reports and hide content that breaks the rules.
  • To hide posts from authors you blocked.

Translation

When a post contains Chinese, our server sends the text to a configured language-model provider to produce an English translation. The translation is stored with the post. The provider processes the text only to return the translation. We do not sell your content.

Sharing

Posts are public. Anyone using the app can read them. We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. We may disclose content if required by law.

Storage and deletion

Content is stored on our server. You can delete your own posts in the app; deleted posts are removed from the database and no longer shown. Reports are kept for moderation records. To delete your account and all associated data, email us.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

We use HTTPS in production. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not post information you would not want to be public.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.

一件 — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-24

「一件」是一个公开的小社区：写下影响你最大的一个人、一件事，或一段文字，再加一句感悟。

我们收集什么

  • 你自己填写的化名。
  • 一个由设备随机生成的标识，用来保持登录。
  • 你发布的内容：类型、涉及者的化名（如有）、经过、以及一句感悟。
  • 你提交的举报和你屏蔽的作者。
  • 内容与举报的创建时间。

我们不收集真实姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、精确位置、照片或广告标识。

用途

  • 向其他用户展示内容。
  • 在这台设备上保持登录。
  • 审核举报，隐藏违规内容。
  • 隐藏你屏蔽的作者发布的内容。

翻译

内容含中文时，服务器会把文本发送给已配置的语言模型服务，生成英文译文并随内容保存。服务方仅用于返回译文，我们不出售你的内容。

分享

发布的内容是公开的，使用本应用的人都能看到。我们不出售个人数据，也不与广告商共享。法律要求时可能披露。

存储与删除

内容保存在服务器。你可以在应用内删除自己的内容，删除后会从数据库移除并不再展示。举报记录会保留用于审核。需要删除账号及全部数据，请发邮件。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童数据。

安全

正式环境使用 HTTPS。没有系统是绝对安全的，请不要发布你不希望公开的信息。

变更

政策更新时，顶部的日期会改变。
